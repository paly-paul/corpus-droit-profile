Emergency Protocol

Vessel Arrest: 4-Step Emergency Protocol

We have executed vessel arrests across 12 Indian ports. Our emergency protocol ensures preservation of your claim from first contact to enforcement.

01

Emergency Briefing

24/7 counsel available. We assess arrest viability, jurisdiction, and maritime lien status within the first hour of contact.

02

Pleadings & Caveat

Admiralty suit filed with the High Court. Caveat against release entered. Warrant of arrest sought with or without notice.

03

Sheriff's Arrest

Court-appointed Sheriff or Marshal effects arrest. We coordinate with port authorities for safe berthing and custody of the vessel.

04

Security & Resolution

Letter of Undertaking, P&I Club guarantee, or cash deposit negotiated. Release against adequate security. Claim pursued to judgment or settlement.

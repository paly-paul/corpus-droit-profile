Section 9 — Interim Measures of Protection

Section 9 of the Arbitration & Conciliation Act, 1996 empowers Indian courts to grant interim measures before, during, or after an arbitral proceeding. In the maritime context, this is particularly powerful for securing vessel or cargo before an arbitration can be constituted.

Corpus Droit has successfully obtained Section 9 orders including vessel arrest warrants, cargo attachment, and anti-disposal injunctions — often on an ex parte basis where urgency demands it.

Emergency Section 9 Application

Enforcement of Foreign Awards

India is a signatory to the New York Convention on Recognition and Enforcement of Foreign Arbitral Awards. Under Part II of the Act, foreign awards are enforceable as decrees of the court — subject to limited public policy and due process defences.

We advise foreign award-holders on the most efficient enforcement strategy in India, including vessel arrest as a parallel enforcement mechanism where the debtor has maritime assets in Indian waters.

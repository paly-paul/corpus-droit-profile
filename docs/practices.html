Practice Areas

Specialised legal counsel across the full spectrum of maritime, commercial, and financial law.

Admiralty & Maritime

India's admiralty jurisdiction vests in the High Courts under the Admiralty (Jurisdiction and Settlement of Maritime Claims) Act, 2017. We are practitioners of first resort for vessel arrest, in rem proceedings, and maritime lien enforcement.

  • Vessel arrest and release across all Indian ports
  • In rem and in personam admiralty suits
  • Maritime liens: crew wages, salvage, necessaries
  • Collision, grounding, and casualty response
  • General average and salvage contributions
  • P&I Club liaison and Letter of Undertaking negotiation
Instruct for Vessel Arrest

Charterparties & B/Ls

Charterparty disputes involve layered contractual interpretation, industry custom, and often multi-jurisdictional complexity. Our team advises on the full range of charterparty forms — GENCON, NYPE, BARECON — and bills of lading disputes.

  • Voyage, time, and bareboat charterparty disputes
  • Freight, demurrage, and despatch claims
  • Bill of lading: Hague-Visby, Hamburg Rules
  • Cargo damage, shortage, and contamination
  • Dangerous goods liability
  • Off-hire disputes and performance claims
Advise on Charter Dispute

Ship Finance & Mortgages

We advise lenders, shipowners, and lessors on the full lifecycle of ship finance transactions — from mandate to enforcement — with expertise in Indian and international maritime mortgage law.

  • Ship mortgage drafting and registration
  • Priority of maritime liens over mortgages
  • Enforcement of ship mortgages in Indian courts
  • Bareboat charter registration (BBCHP)
  • Asset-based lending documentation
  • Restructuring and work-outs for distressed vessels
Finance Counsel

Banking & SARFAESI

We represent banks, NBFCs, and ARCs in the enforcement of security interests and resolution of non-performing assets, with particular expertise in maritime and commercial asset recovery.

  • SARFAESI Act enforcement for maritime assets
  • DRT and DRAT proceedings
  • IBC insolvency proceedings for shipping companies
  • NCLT and NCLAT maritime insolvency
  • Guarantee enforcement and recovery suits
  • Debt restructuring under RBI frameworks

Corporate & FDI

India's maritime sector involves complex regulatory frameworks — from DG Shipping approvals to FEMA-compliant FDI structures. We advise shipping companies and investors on corporate and regulatory matters.

  • Company formation for Indian shipping entities
  • FDI structuring in maritime and port sectors
  • DG Shipping and MMD approvals
  • Joint ventures and consortium agreements
  • M&A due diligence for fleet acquisitions
  • Sagarmala and port privatisation advisory
Corporate Advisory

Aviation & Tax

Our aviation practice covers aircraft finance, leasing, and regulatory compliance. We also advise on the customs, GST, and direct tax implications of maritime and aviation transactions.

  • Aircraft lease structuring and DGCA approvals
  • Cape Town Convention rights in India
  • Air cargo disputes and AWB claims
  • Customs and IGST on vessel imports
  • GST on maritime services and freight
  • Transfer pricing for shipping companies
